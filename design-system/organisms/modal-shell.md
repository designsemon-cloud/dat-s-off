# Organism → Modal Shell

**Components**: [`AccountKit.tsx`](../../src/components/uxcc/AccountKit.tsx), the one shell and parts behind [`AuthModal`](../../src/components/uxcc/AuthModal.tsx), [`CreditsModal`](../../src/components/uxcc/CreditsModal.tsx) and [`BuyCreditsModal`](../../src/components/uxcc/BuyCreditsModal.tsx). Any new modal uses it too.

Adapted from cult-ui's Halo set (halo-input, halo-field, halo-segmented, halo-badge), rebuilt for our stack and design system: the recessed field with an inset rim, the sliding segmented thumb and the quiet pill chips, without the rainbow glows. Greys first; orange only for focus, the one primary button and errors (Nov 2026).

## Rules

- Footer CTA is always `GlowButton size="lg"`, full width. One per modal.
- Inputs sit one surface step below the card (`bg-bg-secondary` on `bg-bg-tertiary`).
- Option pickers are `ChoiceCard`s in a `role="radiogroup"`, never bespoke cards. Badges are `Chip`s, never emoji.
- Sign in puts Google first (see [`patterns/credit-and-auth-gating.md`](../patterns/credit-and-auth-gating.md)).
- `CreditsModal` and `BuyCreditsModal` still overlap in purpose and neither takes payment yet; which one stays is a product decision (`known-issues.md`).
