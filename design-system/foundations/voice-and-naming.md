# Foundation → Voice and naming

## Positioning

**Design review for AI builders.** Dats Off. is _the design review your AI coding tool never did_. It's written for vibe coders and developers first: people who ship UI with Cursor, v0, Lovable or Claude and have no designer on call. Designers can use it, but the copy never leads with them.

Three things set it apart, and copy should come back to them:

1. **Whole flows, not one screen.** It walks the journey and finds the dead ends between screens.
2. **Fix, re-check, repeat.** It stays until the issue is fixed, and proves it.
3. **A prompt to paste, and the why.** Every fix is ready for the AI tool you already use, and names its principle with a short video or article.

("Pinned to your screen" is table stakes; don't lead with it.)

## Names

| Thing                    | Name                                                                                             | Not                       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| What you run             | a **review** ("New review", "5 free reviews", "Review yours")                                    | critique, audit, analysis |
| The result document      | **What's off** (flow: "What's off in this flow"): the tile in the chat, the panel title, exports | UX critique, report       |
| The step to fix          | a **fix prompt** ("Copy fix prompt")                                                             | code, suggestion          |
| The library of past runs | **Library**, "Search reviews"                                                                    | history                   |

"What's off" plays on the brand (Dats Off.) and reads as a hook, not a category: it says what you get.

"UX critique" appears once, on purpose: in "Not another UX critique tool.", where it names the category we're not.

## Voice

- Plain words a developer uses. Name the principle, then say it in one plain sentence.
- Lead with the outcome ("see what's off", "paste the fix"), not the method.
- Senior designer as the bar ("reads your screen like a senior designer would"), never as the audience.
- No hype words (revolutionary, magic, AI-powered). The AI is the tool they already use; we're the review.

## Key lines

- Hero: "The design review your AI never did."
- Meta title: "Dats Off. — The design review your AI never did". OG: "Design review for AI builders".
- Why: "Not another UX critique tool." / "Your AI writes the code fast, and nobody reviews the design. Critique tools hand you a report and leave. Dats Off. stays until it's fixed."
- Start page: "What did you build?"
- Closing: "Something's off. Find out what."
